# Attribution

The following briefs adapt material from [Matt Pocock's code-review skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/d81f3a183412e71a5b1e84ca21bc1a35eea03a60/skills/engineering/code-review/SKILL.md), used under the MIT license below:

- `verifier.md`: the falsification checks and proof standard of its evidence standard, which draws on the _Evidence-first PR review_ artifact.
- The shared reviewer rules in `reviewerPrompt` (`../reviewers.ts`): its falsification checks, condensed.
- `reviewers/standards.md`: its Standards brief and standards checklist, including the smell baseline after Martin Fowler's _Refactoring_.
- `reviewers/spec.md`: its Spec brief.
- `reviewers/design.md`: its Design brief and design rubric, which adapts John Ousterhout's _A Philosophy of Software Design_.
